How it works

Park in 3 simple steps

No apps to navigate, no phone calls, no cash. Just open SOMO and park.

1
📍

Find nearby spots

Open the app — SOMO instantly shows verified parking spaces within 500m of your location with real-time slot availability.

2
📱

Book & pay instantly

Select your vehicle type, choose a duration, and pay securely via UPI, card, or your SOMO Wallet. Confirmation in seconds.

3
🅿️

Show OTP & park

Share the 4-digit OTP with the parking attendant on arrival. No paperwork, no waiting. Your spot is held for you.

Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Built for Indian drivers who are tired of circling blocks looking for parking.

🗺️

Live Map View

See all available spots on a live map. Colour-coded by availability so you know exactly where to go.

🚗

All Vehicle Types

Car, Bike, Cycle, or Truck — filter spots by your vehicle and see pricing tailored to you.

💳

Secure Payments

Pay via UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking through Razorpay's PCI-compliant gateway.

👛

SOMO Credits

Earn credits on refunds and cashback. Use them for instant payment on future bookings. Credits never expire.

⚡

EV Charging

Filter for spots with EV chargers. Charge your car while it's parked — no extra stops needed.

🧑‍✈️

Valet Service

Can't find the spot yourself? Book valet parking and let the attendant handle it for you.

📊

Booking History

Full history of all your parkings with receipts, duration, and payment details — always accessible.

🔔

Push Notifications

Get notified when your booking is confirmed, when time is running low, and when payment is received.

⚖️

Dispute Resolution

Raise a dispute within 48 hours if something goes wrong. Our team mediates and resolves it fairly.
