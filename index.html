FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first booking.

Is SOMO free to use?
Yes. SOMO is completely free to browse and book. You only pay for the parking itself — there are no hidden platform fees or subscriptions for customers.
Do I need to download an app?
No download needed. SOMO works right in your browser — just tap “Use App” and you’re in. An Android app is also available on Google Play if you prefer.
How much does parking cost in Hyderabad?
Parking rates start from ₹20/hr for bikes and ₹40/hr for cars. Prices vary by location and are always shown upfront before you book — no surprises.
How do I pay for parking?
Pay securely with UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or any debit/credit card via Razorpay’s PCI-compliant gateway. You get an instant booking confirmation and digital receipt.
Can I cancel a booking?
Yes. Cancel before your slot begins for a full refund. Cancellations within 15 minutes of check-in get a 100% refund; within 30 minutes get 70%. Refunds process within 5–7 business days.
Are the parking spots safe and verified?
Every spot on SOMO is verified by our team before it goes live. We check access, safety, and signage so you always know exactly what to expect when you arrive.
